Chapter 11 · Meet the Arduino Family

Looking Ahead

Book page 224

This chapter has given you a broader picture of the types of hardware available, including a breadboard Arduino that you build yourself. You’ve seen the parts that make up the Arduino design, and you’ve seen how to build your own Arduino using a solderless breadboard. You now know how to make more than one Arduino-based prototype without having to purchase more boards. You also know about the variety of Arduino boards on the market, and you should be able to select the Arduino board that best meets your needs. Finally, you’ve gained an understanding of the Arduino open source movement itself.

In the next chapter you’ll learn to use a variety of motors and begin working on your own Arduino-controlled motorized tank!
