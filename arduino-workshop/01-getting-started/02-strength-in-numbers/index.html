Chapter 1 · Getting Started

Strength in Numbers

Book page 6

The Arduino platform increases in popularity every day. If you’re more of a social learner and enjoy class-oriented situations, search the Web for “Cult of Arduino” to see what people are making and to find Arduino-related groups. Members of Arduino groups introduce the world of Arduino from an artist’s perspective. Many group members work to create a small Arduinocompatible board at the same time. These groups can be a lot of fun, introduce you to interesting people, and let you share your Arduino knowledge with others.
